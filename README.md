# game-payment-analysis


This document presents an in-depth, multi-layered analysis of user payment behaviors across two games: Funny Squirrel and Funny Squirrel in Space. It includes strategic insights derived from actual transaction data, covering everything from technical reliability to user experience quality.

📌 Report Highlights
Game-Level Flow Overview: End-to-end view of the payment funnel — from incomplete attempts and rejections to successful payments and refunds.

Rejection Ratio Analysis: Identifies high-risk payment systems and countries, especially where failed transactions outnumber successes.

Monthly Trends: Explores temporal dynamics in payment success, system declines, and refund rates across five months.

Country Insights: Pinpoints top-performing and low-performing countries in terms of payment success and failure rates.

Payment System Performance: Benchmarks the most and least reliable payment providers with key KPIs like PS Decline Rate and Refund Rate.

Strategic Recommendations: Actionable suggestions for product, fraud, and engineering teams — including system audits, UI/UX enhancements, and fallback payment options.

✅ Key Takeaways
Month 4 (April) had the highest transaction volume and the most performance spikes.

Funny Squirrel handles more volume, while its sequel shows a cleaner transaction funnel.

UniPay and WeChat identified as best-performing systems; Athleta flagged as highly problematic.

Certain countries like Turkey, Brazil, and Zimbabwe require targeted investigation due to high failure/refund rates.

This PDF provides stakeholders with a detailed diagnostic tool for optimizing global payment performance and ensuring user trust in high-volume environments.
